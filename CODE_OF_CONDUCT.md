# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We commit to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

## Expected Behavior
Be constructive, inclusive, professional, and considerate of privacy.

## Unacceptable Behavior
Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and publication of private information are not tolerated.

## Enforcement
Report concerns privately to project maintainers. Reports are reviewed promptly and may result in warnings, content removal, or participation restrictions.
